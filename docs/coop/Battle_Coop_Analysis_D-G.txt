**Exp Share freeze bug** – In vanilla Emerald the bug appears when both battlers have `gBattleControllerExecFlags & 0x80` set simultaneously during EXP distribution. The Quetzal ROM **does not modify** this logic; the bug is therefore still present. No new patch was found.
